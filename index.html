How your score is calculated

Four KPIs, each weighted. Maximum score is 100.

KPI points = (your result ÷ best result among all affiliates) × KPI weight
Score = Net deposit pts + FTD pts + Active client pts + SpreadPay pts

Competition conditions

Open to all Weltrade affiliates and applied the same way to everyone.

  1. Who can take part. The competition is open to every Weltrade affiliate.
  2. Scoring window. Only activity from 25 Nov 2026 00:00 to 31 Dec 2026 23:59 (UTC+2) counts.
  3. Client attribution. An affiliate's totals are the sum of the clients they referred. Each client counts once.
  4. Net deposit = deposits minus withdrawals of your clients in the window, in USD. A negative total scores 0 points.
  5. FTD = a client's first-ever deposit, made inside the window.
  6. Active client = a client who placed at least one trade in the window.
  7. SpreadPay = spread-based payout generated by your clients' trading in the window, in USD.
  8. Relative scoring. The leader in each KPI earns its full weight. Everyone else earns a share in proportion to the leader.
  9. Selection. At close, the top 3 scores closest to 100 are selected. Ties go to more FTDs, then higher net deposit.
  10. Daily updates. Standings refresh once a day. Final results are confirmed after data validation.
